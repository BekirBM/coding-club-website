Welcome to Code Crafters

Code Crafters is the student coding club for anyone who is curious about programming, whether you have never written a line of code or you already ship your own apps. We meet every week to learn new languages and tools, work on projects together, and help each other solve problems.

Our members come from every department, not only computer science. Designers, engineers, business students and scientists all bring different ideas to the table, and that mix is what makes our projects interesting. You will leave each session knowing something you did not know before, and you will have friends to build with.

What the Club Offers

  • Weekly beginner-friendly workshops on Python, JavaScript and web development
  • Team hackathons with prizes and mentors
  • Mentorship from senior students and industry guests
  • A shared GitHub organization to host member projects
  • Coding-interview practice and competitive programming sessions
  • A friendly online community for questions between meetings
